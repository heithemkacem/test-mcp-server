User story 1: MCP Agent which can get the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) documents shared below from Website: https://www.esma.europa.eu/  for Document Summarization and comparison in a specified Bucket

· MiFID II (Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II) and MiFIR (Markets in Financial Instruments Regulation): These are cornerstones of EU financial markets regulation, covering everything from trading venues to investor protection.

· Prospectus Regulation: Governs the information provided to investors when securities are offered to the public or admitted to trading on a regulated market.

· Market Abuse Regulation (MAR): Aims to prevent market abuse (insider trading, market manipulation) across EU financial markets.

· Market in Crypto Assets Regulation (MiCA): New regulation for crypto-assets.
